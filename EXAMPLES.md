# What to ask SIL

Everything here is coffee: beans, grinders, espresso machines and the gear around them. That is what SIL carries today, in the US, the UK and Greece. Ask for anything else and your agent says so; nothing is made up to fill the gap.

Your agent's words will differ. The facts come from SIL: the products, the specs, the shops, the prices, the guides. A price is what SIL read on the shop's page, dated; the agent re-reads it before you buy.

```text
you    register me on sil
agent  Opens a browser sign-in. Done once.

you    who am I on sil?

```

## It asks what decides the buy

```text
you    I'm getting into espresso. What grinder should I start with?

agent  For espresso the grinder matters more than the machine. Three things
       decide it: how fine it goes and in what small steps, because espresso
       needs tiny adjustments; the burr set, which sets how even the grind
       is; and whether you'll also brew filter, which needs a coarse setting
       too. Two questions: hand or electric, and will it do both?
```
